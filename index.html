Tools & Dashboards

  • 📈 Portfolio Fundamentals — BABA / GOOG
    Investment dashboard tracking Alibaba and Alphabet fundamentals: 16+ quarters and 11 fiscal years of revenue, margins, EPS, free cash flow, segment revenue, and capital returns — every figure curated from SEC filings and IR releases with dual independent verification. Indexed price performance, TTM P/E history, USD/CNY-aware currency toggle across mismatched fiscal calendars, and a private holdings panel that lives entirely in browser localStorage. Prices refresh daily via launchd; new earnings filings trigger automated curation.
  • 🏃 COROS Training Dashboard
    Personal Strava-style training dashboard built from COROS watch data — personal-best 1K/3K/5K/10K splits, run pace and monthly-volume trends, and strength sessions grouped by routine. Geographic route segments are mined from GPS tracks: repeated runs are clustered by trailhead and aligned onto a shared corridor, then the outbound leg, return leg, and full round trip are each timed through fixed gates (Strava-style segment bests) and drawn on Leaflet maps. Auto-syncs within minutes of a new activity via a 5-minute COROS poller.
  • 🧬 LLM & Multimodal Architecture Atlas
    A browsable gallery of modern LLM, vision-language, and generative-model architectures — model cards with total/active parameters, layers, hidden size, attention type (MHA/GQA/MLA/sparse/hybrid), MoE routing, normalization, and positional encoding, plus a generated architecture diagram per model and side-by-side compare. Each diagram now opens up the attention mechanism itself: exploded, source-verified panels of the real internal dataflow — MLA latent caches, DeepSeek-V4 CSA/HCA compressed-sparse stacks and mHC residuals, DSA/IndexShare token selection, Kimi K3 KDA delta-rule linear attention and AttnRes, Gated DeltaNet, MiniMax MSA block-sparse, sliding/global spans, GQA head-grouping, and MMDiT joint attention. Covers 43 models from GPT-2 to DeepSeek V4, Kimi K3, LongCat-2.0, MiniMax H3 (video gen), Llama 4, Qwen3.5, Inkling, Solar Open 2, gpt-oss, GLM-5.2, Kimi K2.7, MiniMax-M3, Laguna, Gemma 3, and FLUX; specs verified against tech reports and open model cards. For researchers and infra engineers doing quick lookups.
  • 📝 HotCRP Paper Tracker
    Personal dashboard that pulls HotCRP review-system notifications from Gmail (IMAP), groups events by (conference, paper #), and renders per-paper status — submitted, reviews received, accepted, camera-ready, award — with author lists, event timelines, update/comment/review counts, search, and per-status filters. Refreshed automatically by the 4-hour email cron.
  • 📚 Schoology Tracker
    Personal dashboard that pulls Schoology Weekly Summary emails from Gmail (IMAP), parses grades, assignments, and overdue items, and renders a color-coded view per student with sortable courses, sparkline trends from a daily snapshot history, and an activity timeline. Pipeline auto-deploys to GitHub Pages after each refresh.
  • Revenue-per-Chip-Hour Roofline Simulator
    Interactive React dashboard that maximizes per-chip total-token TPM (the policy-invariant proxy for revenue/chip-hour) under TTFT/TPOT SLOs for MoE LLMs. Six models (DeepSeek V4 Pro/Flash, Qwen 3.5-397B, Kimi K2.6, GLM 5.1, MiniMax M2.7) × six chips (B200, B300, GB300 NVL72, TPU Ironwood, TPU v8i, AMD MI355X) × four scenarios, with TPOT/TTFT/cache-hit-rate sliders, optimal-θ search, decode/TTFT breakdowns, and the precision-aware hierarchical roofline algorithm from the companion paper.
